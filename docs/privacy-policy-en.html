Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

🔒 In Short: Meccanen Prayer Times application does not collect, sell, or share your personal data with third parties. All data is stored exclusively on your device.

1. Collected Information

Meccanen Prayer Times stores the following information only on your device (localStorage):

  • Selected or detected city/location info (latitude, longitude, time zone)
  • Prayer time calculation method preference
  • Theme and appearance preferences
  • Notification preferences

This information is never sent to any server and is used solely for the proper functioning of the application.

2. External Services Used

The application uses the following third-party APIs:

  • AlAdhan API (aladhan.com) — For prayer time calculations. Only latitude, longitude, and date information are sent to this service. It contains no personal data.
  • Open-Meteo Geocoding API — For manual city search. Only the search query text is sent to this service.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim API — When location permission is granted, used to translate your GPS coordinates into a readable city/country name. Only latitude and longitude are sent to this service.

3. Location Information

If you grant permission, the application accesses your device's GPS/location service to automatically determine your prayer times. This permission is optional; if you decline it, you can manually search for and select your city instead. Your location is used only for prayer time calculation and place-name lookup (via the AlAdhan and Nominatim services listed above), is stored on your device, and is never permanently retained on any server or shared with third parties.

4. Qibla Compass and Motion Sensor

When you use the Qibla feature, the application accesses your device's magnetic/motion sensors (compass). This sensor data is processed only momentarily, on your device, to display the Qibla direction on screen; it is never recorded or sent off your device.

The app also displays one of the 99 Names of Allah (Esma-ul Husna), based on İbn-i Îsâ-yı Saruhânî's classical 7-planet/7-time-segment system (1541); the name shown is calculated locally on your device according to the day of the week and time of day, independent of prayer times. This content is entirely static, uses no personal data, and does not connect to any server.

If you choose the adhan sound for notifications, the app plays a 3:47 audio recording bundled inside the app. This recording is a self-performed, self-recorded field recording by "bagustris", obtained via Freesound/Pixabay under its stated free-use terms; it involves no data collection or internet connection.

5. Notifications

If you enable prayer time notifications, the application uses Android system notifications. Notification permission is used exclusively for the following purposes; no marketing or promotional notifications are ever sent:

  • Prayer time reminders — a notification is sent a chosen number of minutes before a prayer time and/or exactly when it begins.
  • Status notification (optional) — when enabled, a single, silent, continuously-updated notification shows which prayer time you are currently in and when the next one begins. This notification contains no location or personal data, only the calculated prayer time itself.

To ensure notifications fire at the correct time, on Android 12 and above the app uses the "Alarms & Reminders" (exact alarm) permission. This permission is used solely to schedule prayer time notifications and for no other purpose.

6. Donations

Meccanen Prayer Times is completely free; all themes, prayer locations, and features are open and equal to everyone. For those who wish to support the app, the app includes optional donation links to Ko-fi and PayPal. These links take you out of the app to the respective external site; your payment information is processed by those platforms, and the application never has access to it.

7. Advertisements (AD-FREE)

Meccanen Prayer Times application does not contain any advertisements. No ad networks or tracking SDKs are used.

8. Children's Privacy

The application does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. Parents or legal guardians can contact us if they have any concerns.

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Important changes will be published on this page. The update date is indicated at the top of the page.
